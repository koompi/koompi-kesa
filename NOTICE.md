# Attribution

KOOMPI KESA (`kesa`) is a fork of [pi_agent_rust](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/pi_agent_rust)
by Jeffrey Emanuel, taken at commit `9d4872a0` (11 August 2026).

The fork is maintained independently and does not track upstream releases.
Comments in the source still cite upstream issues as `pi_agent_rust#NNN`.
Those references are kept deliberately: they point at the discussions where the
surrounding behaviour was decided, and rewriting them would leave the comments
saying nothing.
